Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026 — draft, pending legal review before launch.

Coinly ("we", "the app") is a personal expense and income tracker for Android. This page explains what data the app handles and how. Since Coinly hasn't launched publicly yet, this policy will be finalized before the Play Store release.

Data stored on your device

All of your wallets, transactions, categories, and budgets are stored locally on your device using a local database. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable backup.

Google Drive backup (optional)

  • Backup is off by default and only activates if you sign in and enable it.
  • Backups are written to your own Google Drive account, inside a private, hidden application-data folder that only Coinly can access — not your visible Drive files.
  • We request the narrowest Google Drive scope available (drive.appdata), which cannot see or touch any other file in your Drive.
  • Coinly has no servers of its own — we never receive or store a copy of your backup.

Sign-in

If you choose to sign in with Google, we use Google's Credential Manager to authenticate you. We only use this to identify your account for backup/restore — we do not use it for advertising or share it with third parties.

Analytics & advertising

Coinly does not currently include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated first.
