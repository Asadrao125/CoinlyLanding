Delete your account & data

Last updated: August 5, 2026

You can delete your Coinly account and everything associated with it at any time, directly from inside the app. No email, no waiting, and no need to ask us.

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Coinly and go to the More tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  3. Read the confirmation, tick “I understand this can’t be undone”, then tap Delete Account.

Deletion happens immediately. The app signs you out and returns to its first-run state, as though it had just been installed.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Wallets, transactions, categories, budgetsPermanently deleted from your device
Your Google Drive backupPermanently deleted from your Drive
Receipt photos saved in the appPermanently deleted from your device
Your name, email and profile photoPermanently deleted from your device
Purchase record (if you subscribed)Unlinked from this device — see below

This cannot be undone. Coinly has no servers, so there is no copy of your data anywhere for us to restore from. Once your device data and your Drive backup are gone, they are gone. If you only want to switch accounts or free up space, use Log Out instead — that clears the device but leaves your Drive backup intact so you can restore it later.

Your Google account itself

Deleting your Coinly account does not touch your Google account. Coinly only signs you in and removes its own hidden backup folder from your Drive. You can also revoke Coinly's access at any time from your Google account permissions page.

Subscriptions and purchases

Deleting your account unlinks any Coinly purchase from this device, but it does not cancel a subscription — subscriptions are managed by Google Play, not by us. To cancel, go to Google Play → Subscriptions. Google retains its own billing records under its privacy policy, which we have no control over.

If you've already uninstalled the app

Uninstalling deletes everything Coinly stored on your device, but it does not remove a Google Drive backup, because that lives in your own Drive. To remove it, either reinstall Coinly, sign in, and use Delete Account, or remove Coinly's hidden app data directly from Google Drive settings under “Manage apps”.

Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app for any reason, email raoasad125@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject “Coinly account deletion request”. We'll confirm within 7 days and talk you through removing anything that remains.

Because Coinly stores nothing on its own servers, there is generally nothing for us to delete on our side — the data is on your device and in your own Drive, both of which the steps above put entirely under your control.
