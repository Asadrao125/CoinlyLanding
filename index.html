Custom categories, organized your way

Budgets that watch a pool of categories

A Coinly budget isn't tied to one category — group Entertainment + Dining into a single "Discretionary" pool, weekly or monthly, scoped to one wallet or all of them.

  • Alerts screen tells you when you're near or over
