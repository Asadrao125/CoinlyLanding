01

Every wallet its own currency, one dashboard to see it all

Keep a PKR wallet for daily spending and a USD wallet for savings — each currency is locked in at creation, so it never gets mixed up with old transactions. Set a Base Currency once, and Coinly converts every wallet into it automatically using real, daily-refreshed exchange rates.

  • Each wallet's currency is locked forever, so nothing can drift
  • Give each wallet its own icon and colour, so you can tell them apart at a glance
  • Live "≈ amount/day" spend-rate for the days left in the month
  • "All Wallets" converts everything to your Base Currency instantly

02

Custom categories, organized your way

A full set of expense and income categories out of the box, each with its own icon and color — toggle any of them off, and every list respects it instantly.

  • Separate expense and income category lists
  • Your own icon and color per category
  • Live transaction counts, right in the list
Every category, on or off
Pick its icon and colour
03

Budgets that watch a pool of categories

A Coinly budget isn't tied to one category — group Entertainment + Dining into a single "Discretionary" pool, weekly or monthly, always scoped to one wallet so its progress is never blended across currencies.

  • Live progress hint while you're still adding the transaction
  • Tap any budget card to expand its per-category breakdown in place
  • Alerts screen tells you when you're near or over
Create a budget
Watch it in one place
04

Every transaction, one searchable ledger

Timeline groups everything by day with a running daily total, filters down to Income, Expense, or just your recurring entries, and shows attached receipts right in the list — swipe to delete.

  • Day-grouped ledger with per-day totals
  • Filter by All, Income, Expense, or Recurring
  • Attached receipts show inline
05

Set it once, Coinly remembers

Fast enough for a one-off expense — quick-reuse chips, a receipt photo, a live budget hint before you even save — and smart enough to handle the ones that repeat. Toggle "Repeat this transaction," pick daily, weekly or monthly, and Coinly logs it automatically from then on — including any occurrences you missed while the app was closed.

  • Recurring expenses or income — daily, weekly or monthly
  • Set an end date, or leave it running until you say stop
  • Attach a receipt from camera or gallery
